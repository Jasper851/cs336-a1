Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue loved to play with her friends in the park. One day, Sue saw a big, red ball in the park. She wanted to play with it, so she took it from her mom.
Sue took the ball to the park. She saw a boy named Tom playing with a ball. Sue looked at Sue and said, "Can I play with you, but first, you must wash me first." Tom did not like this, but he did it anyway.
Sue's mom saw that Tom was dirty. She said, "Sue, that is not nice. You should put the ball away." Sue thought about it and decided to try. She went back to the park and saw Tom's mom waiting for her. Sue was happy that Tom took her ball. They both laughed and played together.
