Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Sue. Sue liked to play with her ball in her room. She would kick it, jump, and roll it all around. One day, Sue saw a new ball in her box. It was red and shiny, and she thought it looked new.
Sue wanted to play with the new ball, but she could not resist it. So, she asked her mom for help. Together, they went to the box and opened it. Inside the box, they found many colorful balls. Sue was so happy and they played with the balls all day.
At night, Sue took the new ball to her new house. She had a great time playing with it. She even tried to share her toys with her friends. Sue was so proud of her original ball. At night, she showed it off to her mom. Her mom was very happy too, because Sue had a fun story with her big, red ball. They all laughed and played together, and Sue learned that sharing her ball with her friends made the best friends.
