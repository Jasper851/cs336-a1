Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play with her toys and run around the house. One day, she found a big box in her room. It was a mystery box. Lily was very curious about the box.
Lily wanted to see what was inside the box. She tried to open it, but it was too hard. She felt sad. She asked her mom for help. Her mom showed her how to open the box.
Lily opened the box and found a small toy car inside. She was so happy! She played with the car all day long. From that day on, Lily always played with her toys and had lots of fun.
